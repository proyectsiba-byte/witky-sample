Natural Numbers

Natural numbers are used to count objects and describe non-negative whole-number quantities.

Definition

In this wiki, the natural numbers include zero:

"N = {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, ...}"

Some mathematical conventions exclude zero from the natural numbers. This wiki explicitly includes it.

Examples

- "0": zero objects
- "1": one object
- "5": five objects
- "100": one hundred objects

Basic Properties

- The sum of two natural numbers is a natural number.
- The product of two natural numbers is a natural number.
- Subtracting one natural number from another does not always produce a natural number.
- Dividing one natural number by another does not always produce a natural number.

For example, "3 - 5 = -2", which is not a natural number under this convention.
