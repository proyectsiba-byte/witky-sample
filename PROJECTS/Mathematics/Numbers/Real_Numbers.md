Real Numbers

Real numbers include all rational and irrational numbers. They can be represented as points on the number line.

The set of real numbers is represented by "R".

Examples

- "-5"
- "0"
- "2/3"
- "√2"
- "π"

Classification

Every real number is either rational or irrational.

The rational numbers include the integers and natural numbers:

"N ⊆ Z ⊆ Q ⊆ R"

Irrational numbers are also part of "R", but not part of "Q".

Properties

Real numbers can be compared using order relations such as "<", ">", and "=".

The usual arithmetic operations are defined for real numbers, except division by zero.

Number Intervals

Intervals describe ranges of real numbers.

Examples:

- "[1, 5]": includes both endpoints.
- "(1, 5)": excludes both endpoints.
