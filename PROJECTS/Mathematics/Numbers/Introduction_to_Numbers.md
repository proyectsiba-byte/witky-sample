Introduction to Numbers

Numbers are mathematical objects used for counting, measuring, ordering, and describing quantities.

Major Number Systems

Symbol| Name| Description| Examples
"N"| Natural numbers| Non-negative counting numbers under this wiki's convention| "0, 1, 2, 3"
"Z"| Integers| Positive and negative whole numbers, including zero| "-3, -1, 0, 4"
"Q"| Rational numbers| Numbers expressible as a ratio of integers with nonzero denominator| "1/2, -3, 0.75"
—| Irrational numbers| Real numbers that are not rational| "√2, π"
"R"| Real numbers| Rational and irrational numbers together| "-2, 1/3, √2"

Inclusion Relationships

Every natural number is an integer. Every integer is rational, because it can be expressed as a fraction with denominator "1". Every rational number is real.

"N ⊆ Z ⊆ Q ⊆ R"
