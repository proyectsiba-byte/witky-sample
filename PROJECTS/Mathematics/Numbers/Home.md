Relationships Between Number Systems

Using the conventions of this wiki:

"N ⊆ Z ⊆ Q ⊆ R"

Where:

- "N": Natural numbers
- "Z": Integers
- "Q": Rational numbers
- "R": Real numbers

Irrational numbers are real numbers that are not rational.
